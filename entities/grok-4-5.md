# Grok 4.5

**Grok 4.5** is xAI/SpaceXAI's July 2026 coding-and-agents-focused frontier model, notable for a [[coding-agent-evaluation]] story centered on cost efficiency rather than outright benchmark dominance.

## Launch facts

- xAI/SpaceXAI launched Grok 4.5 as its first model trained specifically for coding and agents. [raw/newsletters/ainews-2026-07-09-ainews-spacexai-launches-grok-4-5-first-opus-class-model-post-cursor-a.md:21-24]
- Cursor partnered in training and distribution, and Grok 4.5 became available through Grok Build/API/Cursor with broader ecosystem support. [raw/newsletters/ainews-2026-07-09-ainews-spacexai-launches-grok-4-5-first-opus-class-model-post-cursor-a.md:25-28]
- Reported pricing was $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output tokens, with a 500k context window at launch and discussion of a return to 1M. [raw/newsletters/ainews-2026-07-09-ainews-spacexai-launches-grok-4-5-first-opus-class-model-post-cursor-a.md:38-47]

## Evaluation read

The model landed as a frontier-adjacent cost/performance entry:

- Artificial Analysis ranked it #4 on its Intelligence Index and GDPval-style evals, and placed Grok Build + Grok 4.5 on par with GPT-5.5 Codex in its coding-agent index. [raw/newsletters/ainews-2026-07-09-ainews-spacexai-launches-grok-4-5-first-opus-class-model-post-cursor-a.md:56-70]
- AINews summarized the modal expert take as: not clearly best-in-class overall, but potentially a best buy for many agent orchestrators. [raw/newsletters/ainews-2026-07-09-ainews-spacexai-launches-grok-4-5-first-opus-class-model-post-cursor-a.md:111-117]
- The model's token efficiency became the point: it reportedly used far fewer total tokens per coding-agent task than [[claude-fable-5]] or GPT-5.5 in Artificial Analysis measurements. [raw/newsletters/ainews-2026-07-09-ainews-spacexai-launches-grok-4-5-first-opus-class-model-post-cursor-a.md:67-70]

## Strategic significance

Grok 4.5 strengthens the weekly theme of frontier compression: more labs now offer coding-agent-capable models close enough that economics, harnesses, and distribution matter as much as raw leaderboards.

That makes Grok 4.5 relevant to [[software-factories]] even when it is not the single strongest model: a lower-cost planner/executor can be routed into multi-model workflows where premium models handle only high-judgment steps.

## Caveats

- Skeptics pointed to the 500k context limit, possible long-session degradation, and verbosity/thinking behavior as unresolved harness issues. [raw/newsletters/ainews-2026-07-09-ainews-spacexai-launches-grok-4-5-first-opus-class-model-post-cursor-a.md:118-124]
- The corpus treats Cursor's influence as plausible but not fully proven: hard facts are partnership, training collaboration, and distribution, not a verified causal rescue of xAI's model trajectory. [raw/newsletters/ainews-2026-07-09-ainews-spacexai-launches-grok-4-5-first-opus-class-model-post-cursor-a.md:90-97]
