# Microduck

**Microduck** is a small open-source biped from Pollen Robotics and Hugging Face, reported at about 25 cm and $399. Its significance in the batch is the combination of an affordable physical platform, an open simulator, pre-trained policies, and sim-to-real reinforcement-learning workflows.

## Why it matters

The reported design invites community experimentation rather than treating robotics as a closed demonstration. The robot has 15 actuators and a sensor stack including camera, speaker, LiDAR, NFC, Bluetooth, and Wi-Fi; the sources also describe early sales momentum and rapid community experiments. These details are newsletter-reported rather than independently verified here. [raw/newsletters/ainews-2026-08-28-ainews-openai-to-reach-agi-bar-by-end-2026.md][raw/newsletters/the-neuron-2026-08-30-anthropic-taught-ai-agents-to-use-machines.md]

Microduck is a useful bridge between [[agentic-robotics]], [[real-world-agent-evaluations]], and [[hugging-face]]. It makes the feedback loop—simulate, train, deploy, observe, and improve—available at a scale where more researchers can participate.
