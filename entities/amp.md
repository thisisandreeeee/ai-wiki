# AMP

**AMP** is an AI infrastructure company discussed in Latent.Space’s interview with Anjney Midha. Its core thesis is that frontier AI is limited not only by GPU supply but by utilization, scheduling, reliability, power, and coordination.

## Key idea

AMP wants compute to work more like an electric grid: pooled, scheduled, multi-cloud, multi-silicon, and coordinated by an independent system operator rather than a single vertically integrated lab.

## Signals from the interview

- Best-in-class model FLOPs utilization was described as ~60–70%, while some frontier-scale clusters may be far lower.
- Google-style node utilization targets around 95% were used as a benchmark for operational seriousness.
- AMP’s ambition was framed around base-load and spike capacity for frontier research teams, with interruptible demand and dynamic priority as key scheduling primitives.
- The interview linked compute infrastructure to community consent, power grids, and responsible data-center development.

## Why it mattered

AMP represents the “AI scaling is systems engineering” thread. The corpus repeatedly points away from raw model demos and toward harnesses, schedulers, data pipelines, evals, and reliability layers.
