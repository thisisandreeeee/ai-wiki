# AIUC

**AIUC** (Artificial Intelligence Underwriting Company) is building confidence infrastructure for frontier AI through standards, technical testing, and insurance. Latent.Space describes its thesis as a response to a deployment bottleneck: as agents become more capable and autonomous, enterprise adoption is constrained by liability, risk, and trust rather than capability alone.

## AIUC-1

AIUC-1 is presented as a standard for agent security, safety, and reliability. The proposed framework covers concerns such as jailbreaks, hallucinations, data leakage, and operational controls, with testing based on thousands of simulations and a quarterly refresh cycle. The standard is intended to be grounded in technical evidence rather than paperwork alone. These are company and interview claims, not an independent certification audit. [raw/newsletters/latent-space-2026-09-16-underwriting-superintelligence-backing-agents-you-can-sue-rune-kvist-a.md]

The insurance connection is structural: standards define the tests and controls, while insurers have an incentive to quantify and reduce risk before underwriting deployment. AIUC’s reported customers and ecosystem include Cursor, Harvey, Lovable, and ElevenLabs; the source reports a $40M Series A and a team of about 20 people at the time of the interview.

## Why it matters

AIUC connects [[real-world-agent-evaluations]] to enterprise accountability. A model can perform well in a demo and still fail a bank, hospital, or critical-infrastructure rollout if its permissions, monitoring, escalation, and liability chain are unclear.
