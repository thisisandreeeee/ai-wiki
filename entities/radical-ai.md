# Radical AI

**Radical AI** is a materials-science company highlighted by Latent.Space for building self-driving labs around closed-loop alloy discovery.

## Corpus summary

Joseph Krause described Radical’s thesis: materials discovery cannot be solved by a one-shot model because the manufactured material depends on process variables, microstructure, characterization, and scale-up constraints. The lab itself and its experimental data become the moat.

Reported signals:

- Radical scaled an alloy discovery pipeline to **1,200 alloys in six months**, described as roughly 10x faster than the DARPA/GE MACH reference point.
- Its AI scientist reportedly proposed and tested 300 new materials, with 10 showing novel state-of-the-art properties being developed commercially.
- The company open-sourced parts of its tooling, including TorchSim and MATRIX/MATRIX-PT.

## Why it mattered

Radical AI anchored the corpus’s applied science thread: AI progress in physical domains depends on measurement, robotics, and feedback loops, not just larger foundation models.
