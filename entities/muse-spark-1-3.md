# Muse Spark 1.3

**Muse Spark 1.3** is Meta’s September 2026 workhorse model for agentic and coding tasks, positioned around instruction following, tool use, long-context work, and efficiency.

## Reported position

Meta reported about 20% fewer tool calls and 25% fewer tokens than the prior Spark release. The batch places Spark 1.3 near or above GPT-5.6 Sol on selected evaluations and reports an unusually strong long-context result, while also noting that the model may be too large for many local users and that open weights were still forthcoming. ## Why it matters

Spark 1.3 represents the open-workhorse direction: model quality is paired with fewer wasted steps, possible self-hosting, and a broader agent harness. Its comparison with [[gemini-3-8-flash]] shows why model choice should optimize completed-task cost and latency, not token price or a single leaderboard position.
