What should be the correct reference class? If you want to know whether your income is high, should you compare yourself to all Americans, all Californians, all residents of San Jose, or just your neighborhood?… This question: which comparison group is “right” for assigning a probability to an individual case, is one of the oldest open problems in the foundations of probability.
