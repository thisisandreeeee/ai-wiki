# 😼 OpenAI Launched GPT-5.6 and Turned ChatGPT Into a Desktop Super-App

So, if you’re a ChatGPT user, there’s a high likelihood you just opened the desktop app yesterday or this morning and found a whole new model family waiting for you, along with a totally different vision of what ChatGPT is supposed to be.

See, OpenAI just _officially _launched [GPT-5.6](https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-6/), and then surprisingly also rebuilt the app around “ChatGPT for work” to enable “work” that continues across chats, files, browsers, and devices. _And like we said, it’s surprising and upsetting a many engineers. _

**Here's what happened:**

* To review, GPT-5.6 comes in Sol, Terra, and Luna: think of them like most powerful, balanced, and affordable. 

* Sol's Ultra mode coordinates multiple agents on harder jobs.

* The new [ChatGPT Work](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-for-your-most-ambitious-work/) can browse the web, use connected apps, edit files, operate your computer, schedule tasks, and create slides, spreadsheets, documents, and sites easier and better than ever before.

* Codex is moving inside the unified desktop app, while OpenAI [sunsets its early browser experiment Atlas](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/09/openai-is-discontinuing-chatgpt-atlas-its-standalone-desktop-browser/) and shifts those browser features into the app and a Chrome sidebar.

**People's reaction:**

* [Tibo Sottiaux](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2075283734136525255) called Sol OpenAI's best model yet across coding, difficult context, strategy, and site building. [Andrew Curran](https://x.com/AndrewCurran_/status/2075273510331785658) highlighted OpenAI's claim that Sol helped post-train Luna.

* [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/9/gpt-5-6/) praised the capability while documenting a dizzying matrix of three models, multiple effort settings, prices, tool calling, and multi-agent options.

* [Jun Song](https://x.com/jun_song/status/2075051018564653155) called Sol strong but incremental. [Peter Gostev](https://x.com/petergostev/status/2074918176354115886) saw Fable as the “wise owl” for architecture and Sol as the dependable executor.

* And yet: [Theo](https://x.com/theo/status/2075312087723876556), [signüll](https://x.com/signulll/status/2075312075149087159), [Corbin Braun](https://x.com/corbinbraun/status/2075307001182511231), and [Daniel Lockyer](https://x.com/DanielLockyer/status/2075145237081539008) all captured the UX problem: early Codex users liked their more a focused Codex tool and instead received a super-app with more modes & UI changes to understand. And yet [Sriram Krishnan](https://x.com/sriramk/status/2075230530841215272) argued that this super-app is where the market is heading.

**How to try it:**

* Update or download the ChatGPT desktop app.

* Pick ChatGPT for Work or ChatGPT Codex based on the job (normal vs coding).

* Choose Sol, Terra, or Luna, then raise effort when the task deserves it.

* To chat, select “Chat” and you can chat anywhere with the floating chat window. 

_Just don’t ask us where your chat threads are now… that part is confusing as all get out. _

**Why this matters:** Grok released a new coding model. Meta just released a new coding model. Gemini 3.5 is supposedly coming out July 17. Presumably, all of them will be cheaper than Fable (Sol is supposedly 1/3 the cost). _This is Anthropic’s game to lose. _

**Our take:** _Let’s talk about UX, bay-beee._ Once again, the UI problem is really a **memory problem.** Everyone is confused about where their chats went in the super-app, which is fair. But surfing through old threads are miserable to reopen and skim. _IF I never had to scroll anything ever again it would be too soon. Can voice / AI UI totally remove scrolling from my life plz? It’s the worst UX ever, IMO. Your information should come TO you now. _

Besides killing scrolling, what would be better UX is really just **one chat. **One chat that remembers everything important about your projects, tools, and decisions, that’s kinda always running and easy to pull up, that can surface info as needed, and sure, maybe it floats around wherever you go. 

That appears to be OpenAI's goal. It is not a bad pattern. It’s just different. But until memory becomes reliable enough to replace a thousand chat threads with one thread to rule them all, the app will continue to feel like _dude where’s my chat_ because somebody rustled all the papers around on your desk and now you can’t find anything.

# 🎓 AI Skill of the Day: Use One Smart Model With Cheaper Workers

Using your smartest model for every token is like hiring a CEO to update every spreadsheet cell. [Anthropic shared two patterns](https://x.com/ClaudeDevs/status/2074606058128224365) for keeping Fable 5 in charge while Sonnet 5 handles most of the expensive, token-heavy work.

1. **Use Fable as an advisor.** Sonnet executes the task, then calls Fable only when it needs strategic guidance or a course correction. Anthropic’s [advisor tool](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/agents-and-tools/tool-use/advisor-tool) gives the advisor the full conversation, then returns its guidance to the executor. On SWE-bench Pro (_a recently criticized coding benchmark)_, this pairing reached about 92% of Fable’s score at roughly 63% of the price.

2. **Use Fable as an orchestrator.** Fable makes the plan and delegates execution to Sonnet sub-agents. Anthropic’s [cookbook](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-cookbooks/blob/main/managed_agents/CMA_plan_big_execute_small.ipynb) shows the “plan big, execute small” setup. On BrowseComp (a browser benchmark), it reached 96% of Fable’s performance at 46% of the price.

So use the advisor pattern for one hard task that occasionally needs steering. Use the orchestrator pattern when the work can be split across [multiple agents](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/managed-agents/multi-agent). Each sub-agent keeps its own cache, so repeated calls do not repay the full context cost.

```
Act as the lead planner. Break this task into clear work packages. Delegate routine, token-heavy execution to lower-cost workers. Keep strategic decisions, quality checks, and course corrections for yourself. Review each worker’s result before producing the final answer.

Task: [PASTE TASK]
```
