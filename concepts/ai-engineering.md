# AI Engineering

**AI engineering** is the applied discipline of building, deploying, evaluating, and steering AI systems in real product and business contexts. The batch reports that Andrew Ng’s DeepLearning.AI relaunched with this focus after analyzing more than 10,000 job postings, interviews, surveys, and online data.

## Four capability areas

The source groups the work into four overlapping skills:

1. **Build and deploy AI applications** using models, context engineering, RAG, agentic workflows, and disciplined evaluation/error analysis.
2. **Apply software-engineering fundamentals** so architecture, data stores, testing, and stack choices remain deliberate rather than opaque consequences of generated code.
3. **Use coding agents effectively** by understanding their limits, steering them, specifying tasks, coordinating multiple agents, and controlling production risk.
4. **Shape the build** with product sense and business context: know when to ship an MVP, when to slow down, and how to connect technical work to customer outcomes. [raw/newsletters/ainews-2026-08-25-ainews-andrew-ng-gets-into-ai-engineering.md]

## Why it matters

The framing raises the ceiling more than it lowers the floor. AI makes implementation cheaper, but expertise remains valuable for choosing trade-offs, supplying context, designing evaluation loops, and deciding what should be built. That connects AI engineering to [[software-factories]], [[coding-agent-evaluation]], and [[agent-experience]].
