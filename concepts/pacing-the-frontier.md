# Pacing the Frontier

**Pacing the frontier** is the proposal that governments and labs should develop technical and governance mechanisms to deliberately slow frontier AI development if automated AI progress becomes too fast to supervise.

## July 2026 trigger

AINews quoted the petition logic directly: industry and governments may need the option to buy time for security measures and oversight, but no single company or country has incentive to slow down unilaterally. Signatories included employees from frontier AI companies, and the discussion was amplified by OpenAI and Anthropic leadership. [raw/newsletters/ainews-2026-07-29-ainews-fearing-rsi-openai-anthropic-gdm-meta-thinky-cosign-letter-to-p.md:19-23]

The same week, The Neuron framed the proposal against the OpenAI/Hugging Face incident and accelerating launch cadence, while the next issue highlighted Mark Zuckerberg's public rejection of the slowdown frame in favor of acceleration and broad distribution. [raw/newsletters/the-neuron-2026-07-30-zuckerberg-split-with-his-own-ai-chief.md:48-62]

## Debate

Supporters treat pacing as a coordination tool for [[recursive-self-improvement]] risk, cyber containment, evaluation transparency, and frontier-lab oversight. Critics treat it as potential regulatory capture: a brake that could entrench closed incumbents, burden open-weight competitors, and fail to constrain international rivals.
