# Model Labs vs Agent Labs

**Model labs vs agent labs** is the strategic split between companies that primarily advance foundation models and companies that build durable systems around models: harnesses, memory, tools, workflows, customer integrations, and operational loops.

## Thesis from the corpus

AINews summarized Sarah Guo’s argument that agent/application companies can be hard to copy when they translate messy private customer reality into model-actionable systems. The “untrainable” part is not just the model; it is integration, maintenance, process change, and context.

June 19 AINews reinforced this: the center of gravity keeps moving from “model” to “model + harness + memory + SCM.” Coding-agent infrastructure needs synchronized worktrees, review state, environments, permissions, and source-control abstractions.

## Why it matters

The best model may not win if the operational harness is unreliable. Conversely, a strong harness can turn multiple models into a usable agent product.
