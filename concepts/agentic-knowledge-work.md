# Agentic Knowledge Work

**Agentic knowledge work** is the use of persistent agents to produce documents, spreadsheets, sites, analyses, plans, and business artifacts across tools rather than only write code.

## July 2026 signal

Latent.Space reported that ChatGPT Work and Codex reached 10M combined users less than two weeks after launch, with Codex powering ChatGPT Work even for non-engineers. The key product claim is that coding-agent infrastructure is moving into broader work: documents, spreadsheets, slide/site-like artifacts, plugins, memory, subagents, local files, Slack, and connected apps. [raw/newsletters/latent-space-2026-07-28-codex-from-0-to-10m-users-building-chatgpt-work-akshay-nathan-openai.md:14-21]

The Neuron added labor-market texture: an OpenAI study found 43.5% of occupation-specific ChatGPT messages involved work normally associated with a different occupation, suggesting AI expands task boundaries before org charts catch up. [raw/newsletters/the-neuron-2026-07-28-nvidia-s-open-ai-counterpunch.md:20-27]

## Why it matters

This is where [[software-factories]] meet ordinary enterprise workflows. The same harnesses that can edit code can now generate financial analyses, dashboards, memos, sites, and review packets. That increases leverage but also requires [[agent-reliability-and-operations]]: provenance, permissions, cost controls, and human review become business-process requirements.
