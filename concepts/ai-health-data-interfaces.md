# AI Health Data Interfaces

**AI health data interfaces** connect personal health records, wearable data, lab results, and medical context to AI assistants for explanation, preparation, and longitudinal review.

## July 2026 signal

The Neuron reported Health in ChatGPT rolling out to logged-in U.S. users age 18+ across major plans, with Apple Health and supported medical records as connectable context. OpenAI said connected data and conversations that use it will not train foundation models or target ads, and the product is meant to organize and explain health information rather than replace care. [raw/newsletters/the-neuron-2026-07-24-chatgpt-can-read-your-medical-records.md:46-80]

AINews separately framed the rollout as a high-trust application layer: connected health data receives extra encryption, physician review shaped the product, and the risk is that a polished answer grounded in real records may feel more authoritative than it deserves. [raw/newsletters/ainews-2026-07-24-ainews-black-forest-labs-flux-3-multimodal-flow-models-that-beat-seeda.md:46-49]
